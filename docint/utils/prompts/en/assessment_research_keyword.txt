- keyword(words): counts every indexed chunk of the collection that contains all of these words, in any order, and returns the most relevant new ones. A word also matches the longer words it begins, so "measure" finds "measurement"; single letters or digits, words split by dots or signs, like 2.5 or U.S., and words over 30 characters cannot be checked. Use it for exact names, numbers, ids and terms a search may miss, and to check whether the material mentions something at all: a count of 0 is exact. Give one to three words, not a sentence. Do not repeat a check.
