- keyword(words, field): counts every indexed chunk of the collection that contains all of these words, in any order, and returns the most relevant new ones. A word also matches the longer words it begins, so "measure" finds "measurement". Use it for exact names, numbers, ids and terms a search may miss, and to check whether the material mentions something at all: a count of 0 is exact. Give one to three words, not a sentence. field is one of {fields}: text is the passage text (the default), author who wrote a posting, network the platform, uuid one posting's id. Do not repeat a check.
